A page for supporting the next title in the series

Sandscram (거북이 경주) is a short game of luck for 2–8 people on one phone. Each person takes a sea turtle hatchling and cheers to see who reaches the sea first.

The race includes natural predators such as gulls and crabs. Nets and plastic are another danger that people left behind. The ending of the game tells that story.

One person made this game in the time between delivery shifts. The links below are for supporting the next title in the series.
